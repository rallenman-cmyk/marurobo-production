## 共通の英語の指示（各案の頭に付ける）
Background art for a YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, bright clean soft 3D illustration like a polished explainer channel, soft daylight, light pastel colors, simple rounded cartoon people with smooth faces. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT anywhere (no letters, numbers, logos). Do NOT draw any black bar. The TOP 20% of the frame is plain background (a title is added later). Keep the bottom-right corner free of important things.
Mascot robot (must match the reference image): a glossy white sphere body, ONE large round camera-lens eye (navy outer ring, teal iris ring, glossy dark pupil), a thin grey antenna with a small orange ball, two short stubby grey legs with grey shoes, a small brown checkered detective cap. No mouth, no arms, no hands, no eyebrows.
